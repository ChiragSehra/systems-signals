# Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

Engineers, researchers, and technically curious builders reading long-form material about AI systems, agent architectures, LLM infrastructure, distributed systems, software architecture, and developer tooling. Their job is to understand difficult ideas, inspect implementation details, and follow a coherent argument without marketing or navigation noise.

### Brand Personality

Academic, precise, calm. The site should evoke confidence, concentration, and intellectual curiosity. It should feel like an online technical book or research notebook rather than a product website.

### Aesthetic Direction

A restrained editorial publication with strong typography, generous whitespace, readable line lengths, clear hierarchy, and excellent code and figure presentation. Support both light and dark themes. Use the JAX Scaling Book as a reference for reading quality, without copying its implementation. Avoid SaaS landing pages, gradients, glassmorphism, card-heavy layouts, decorative animation, and excessive navigation chrome.

### Design Principles

1. Make the writing the dominant visual element.
2. Use typography, spacing, and hierarchy to make complexity easier to follow.
3. Give technical artifacts—code, equations, diagrams, tables, and citations—first-class presentation.
4. Prefer quiet, purposeful interaction over decoration.
5. Adapt the reading experience deliberately for narrow screens rather than merely shrinking desktop layouts.
